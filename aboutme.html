About Me

I am Ruth Moraa, a bioinformatician (MSc) and microbiologist (BSc) finalising a PhD in Evolutionary Genomics at the Institute for Organismic and Molecular Evolution (Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz). My research bridges evolutionary biology and computation, focusing on comparative omics and reproducible analysis workflows for complex genomic datasets.

My PhD research examined chromosome evolution in termites, including non-canonical sex chromosome systems, meiotic multivalents and ring configurations, and novel chromosomal translocations. I focus on how these architectures arise, how they shape genome structure, and how they relate to inbreeding and evolutionary change.
